# Better Tabs Privacy Policy

Better Tabs does not collect, store, or send any personal data to the developer or to any third party.

- Settings and the list of protected sites are stored in your browser's extension storage. If browser sync is on, your browser syncs them through your Firefox or Google account.
- Ping status (last ping time and result) stays on your computer.
- Keepalive pings go only to the sites you protect, and only after you grant access to each site. They carry your existing cookies for that site, exactly like reloading the page yourself.
- Tab URLs and titles are read only to group, sort, and dedupe tabs. They never leave your browser.
- The extension has no analytics, tracking, or remote code.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
